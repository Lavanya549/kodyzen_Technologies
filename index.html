About KodyZen

🚀 KodyZen is a technology-focused freelancing startup dedicated to helping students bridge the gap between academics and industry. Our primary focus is Final Year Engineering Projects, where we guide students in building innovative and industry-relevant solutions across multiple technologies and domains. Along with project development, we help students strengthen their technical skills and prepare for placements through Mock Interviews, Python, Java, SQL training, Resume Building, and Career Mentorship. We believe a project should not just be submitted — it should help students confidently explain their work during interviews and stand out in placements.
